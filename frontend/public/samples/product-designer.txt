Product Designer (Healthcare) — Calmwater Health, Pune

- A portfolio showing end-to-end process, from research to shipped UI.
- Strong interaction design and information architecture skills.
- Experience designing accessible interfaces (WCAG).

Bonus
- Healthcare experience.
- Ability to prototype in code (HTML/CSS or React).
- Experience with quantitative research or product analytics.
